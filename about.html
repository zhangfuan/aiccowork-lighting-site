Who We Are

AICCO WORK Lighting is an automotive LED lighting supplier focused on the wholesale and project market. Our product range covers LED work lights, off-road light bars, vehicle headlight and tail light assemblies, and custom OEM/ODM lighting programs.

We are built around export-first service: from product development and certification support to photography design, customs declaration, logistics tracking and after-sales service. Whether you are an importer, distributor, brand owner or fleet supplier, our team works with you as an extension of your own sourcing department.

What We Do

LED work lightsVehicle and worksite work lights from 220W to 330W, including rectangular replacement-style lamps for trucks and engineering machinery.
Off-road light barsAnti-glare 12V bars and modular 40 / 52.5 inch systems with IP-rated construction for 4x4 and heavy-duty vehicles.
Vehicle assembliesBMW headlight upgrades with DRL and premium OLED tail lights for the aftermarket upgrade segment.
OEM / ODM programsLogo engraving, custom packaging, specific parameters and new design development for brand owners.
