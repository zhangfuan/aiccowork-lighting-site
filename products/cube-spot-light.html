Cube Spot Ditch Light — Product Overview

The cube spot is a best-selling auxiliary light for the off-road and truck market. Mounted on A-pillars or bumpers, it lights the sides of the road — the "ditch" — where long beams don't reach. This hot-sale version adds an amber backlight function for a distinctive look, with an IP68 waterproof build and 6500K output.

Popular on Tacoma, 4Runner and similar builds, it is an easy add-on SKU that pairs with any light bar or work light lineup.
