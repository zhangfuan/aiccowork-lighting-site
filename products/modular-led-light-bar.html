Modular Off-Road LED Bar — Product Overview

The modular off-road LED bar is a flexible lighting solution for B2B buyers supplying 4x4, truck and heavy-duty markets. Instead of stocking many fixed lengths, distributors can cover different builds with a modular system available in 40 and 52.5 inch configurations.

With IP68-rated construction, this heavy-duty bar is built for off-road, worksite and fleet conditions, backed by factory-direct pricing, low MOQ and OEM/ODM customization.
